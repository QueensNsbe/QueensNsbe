How It Works

Get Involved

🔑 Become a Mentor

Take on a leadership role and guide the next generation of Black engineers and computer scientists. Share your experiences, offer advice on academics and careers, and help students navigate challenges with confidence. In the process, you'll grow your own leadership, communication, and networking skills.

Learn More

🌱 Become a Mentee

Join a supportive community where you can learn from upper-year students and professionals who have already walked the path you're on. Gain advice on studying, career opportunities, and personal growth while building meaningful connections that will carry you through university and beyond.

Learn More

Real Connections

Building Lasting Relationships

Our mentorship program pairs first and second-year students with upper-year mentors who've walked the same halls, faced the same courses, and found their footing at Queen's. From coffee chats to résumé reviews, mentorship at NSBE is hands-on and personal.

Whether you're looking for guidance or ready to give back, there's a place for you in the program.

Learn more with our 2025–26 Mentorship Package, or contact us at nsbe.mentorship@engsoc.queensu.ca
