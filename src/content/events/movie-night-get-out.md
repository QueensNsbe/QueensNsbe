The NSBE Movie Night featuring *Get Out* was an exciting mix of fun and
suspense, with plenty of moments that had everyone reacting, some laughing,
some jumping, and some hiding behind their hoodies. The atmosphere was
laid-back and lively, with pizza, wings, and good vibes all around. Beyond
the movie itself, it was a great chance for members to unwind, talk, and
learn more about each other.
