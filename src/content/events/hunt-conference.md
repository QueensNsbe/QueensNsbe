The HUNT Conference at McMaster was an inspiring chance for our NSBE team to
grow professionally and expand our network. We connected with companies like
WSP and Lumentum, learned about internships, and attended workshops on
résumés and professional branding. Members received résumé feedback, took
headshots, and built valuable connections.
