Our NSBE Internship Panel gave members a clear, honest look into internship
opportunities at Queen's. Representatives from QUIP and ECAP shared how the
university supports students, while past interns discussed their real
experiences, what they enjoyed, what they learned, and whether they'd
return to their placements.
