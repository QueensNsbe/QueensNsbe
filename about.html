Our Story

The National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) is one of the largest student-governed organizations in the world, founded in 1975 and now home to over 16,000 members across 500+ chapters worldwide. NSBE's mission is to increase the number of culturally responsible Black engineers who excel academically, succeed professionally, and positively impact the community.

At Queen's University, NSBE provides a vibrant community where Black students in STEM can thrive through academic support, professional development, mentorship, leadership opportunities, and community service. Guided by our core values of Excellence, Exposure, Community, Empathy, and Leadership, we strive to create spaces where every member feels empowered to achieve their goals.

Our Core Pillars Of Impact

At NSBE Queen's University, our pillars reflect our commitment to empowering Black students through leadership, community, and academic success.

Professional Development

Equip members with the tools to succeed in the workplace through alumni talks, networking, and career resources.

Mentorship & Connections

Foster a strong community by connecting students across years through workshops, socials, and mentorship.

Academic Support

Help members thrive academically with study pods, exam prep, and peer tutoring.

A Note From Our Co-Presidents

Welcome to NSBE Queen's University!

At NSBE Queen's University, we are dedicated to fostering a supportive community for Black engineering students. Our mission is to increase the representation of Black engineers and provide resources for personal and professional growth.

We aim to inspire, connect, and empower our members through mentorship, networking, and leadership opportunities. NSBE Queen's University is not just about academic success, but about building a lasting impact on both our members and the communities we serve.

On behalf of the Co-Presidents and the entire executive team, thank you for visiting our page. We invite you to join us as we continue to make strides in shaping the future of engineering!

— Golden Onyekere & Mahuel Bunsie
Co-Presidents, NSBE Queen's University
